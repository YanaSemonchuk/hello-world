## Lab work #2 — residence time distribution

The worksheet processes tracer concentration measurements and fits a tanks-in-series model.

- **Input:** tracer concentration `Cv` measured at 10 time points (t = 0…9)
- **Normalisation:** builds the normalised C-curve from the measurements
- **Mean residence time:** τ ≈ 3.32
- **Model:** tanks-in-series, n = 4
- **Fit quality:** model vs experiment deviation σ ≈ 0.0205

Open with Mathcad 14 or later (Mathcad Prime can convert `.xmcd` files).
